Construction and optical policy

The space between the parts matters.

Every supported 16 px combination is approved by empytree. Optical measurements remain visible as observations; they do not remove a face from the pool. The top-crease frame remains display-only.

Optical observations and the author’s decision

Frame, eyes and mouth are rasterized at 1×/2×/3×. The report retains small gaps, quarter-rest detail and identical thresholded silhouettes as observations. The author accepts every supported micro combination. The current frame fills the canvas. Measurements describe enlarged micro geometry; ICO layers use micro/compact/display according to physical resolution. Observations do not act as automatic exclusions.
